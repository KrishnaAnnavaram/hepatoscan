---
title: Liver lesions in general
source: General patient-education summary written for this project; not clinically reviewed
---
A liver lesion is any area of the liver that looks different from the tissue around it. Many liver lesions are benign (not cancer), for example simple cysts, haemangiomas and focal nodular hyperplasia.

Some lesions are cancer. A cancer can start in the liver (primary liver cancer, for example hepatocellular carcinoma) or spread to the liver from another organ (liver metastasis).

To find out what a lesion is, doctors use the medical history, blood tests, more imaging such as MRI or contrast ultrasound, and sometimes a biopsy. A CT measurement alone does not tell what a lesion is.
