---
title: Questions to ask your doctor
source: General patient-education summary written for this project; not clinically reviewed
---
These questions can help you prepare for an appointment:

- What did the radiologist see on my scan, and what does it mean for me?
- Do I need more tests, and why?
- What are the possible causes of this finding?
- Who will explain the results, and when?
- What signs or symptoms must I report before the next appointment?
- Where can I get reliable written information about my condition?
