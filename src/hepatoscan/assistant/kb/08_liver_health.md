---
title: General liver health
source: General patient-education summary written for this project; not clinically reviewed
---
General advice for liver health includes limiting alcohol, keeping a healthy weight, eating a balanced diet and being active. Vaccination against hepatitis B and testing for viral hepatitis can also protect the liver.

Some medicines, supplements and herbal products can harm the liver. Do not start or stop a medicine because of information from this tool. Ask your doctor or pharmacist first.
