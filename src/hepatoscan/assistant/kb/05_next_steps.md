---
title: Next steps after a scan finding
source: General patient-education summary written for this project; not clinically reviewed
---
If a scan shows a possible liver lesion, your care team decides the next steps. Common next steps are a review by a radiologist, more imaging, blood tests or a referral to a liver specialist (hepatologist) or to a multidisciplinary team.

Treatment options depend on the type of lesion, its size and number, the health of the rest of the liver and your general health. Only your care team can tell which options apply to you.

Keep a copy of your reports, a list of your medicines and a list of your questions for each appointment.
