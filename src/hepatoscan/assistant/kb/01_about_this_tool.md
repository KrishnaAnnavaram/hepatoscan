---
title: What hepatoscan does and does not do
source: hepatoscan project documentation
---
hepatoscan is a research and education tool. It marks the liver region and possible lesions on a CT volume with a computer model. The output is an automatic measurement, not a diagnosis.

A computer model can miss a lesion or mark normal tissue as a lesion. Only a radiologist or another qualified doctor can interpret a CT scan. Always discuss any result with your care team.

hepatoscan does not give treatment advice, medicine doses or a prognosis. For these questions, talk to your doctor, nurse or pharmacist.
