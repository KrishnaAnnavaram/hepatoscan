---
title: Warning signs that need urgent care
source: General patient-education summary written for this project; not clinically reviewed
---
Get urgent medical help, for example from emergency services, if you have any of these signs:

- vomiting blood or material that looks like coffee grounds
- black, tarry or bloody stools
- severe pain in the abdomen
- new confusion, strong sleepiness or fainting
- yellow skin or eyes that appear suddenly, with fever or pain
- difficult breathing or chest pain

Do not wait for an online answer if you have these signs.
