---
title: How to read the hepatoscan numbers
source: hepatoscan project documentation
---
The liver volume is the number of marked liver voxels multiplied by the size of one voxel, in millilitres (mL). The size of a normal adult liver varies much between people.

The lesion count is the number of separate regions that the model marked as possible lesions. The lesion volume is the total volume of these regions. A small marked region can be noise, a cyst, a vessel or a real lesion. The model cannot tell which.

The numbers depend on the scan quality, the slice thickness and the model. Two scans of the same person can give different numbers. Your doctor can compare the result with earlier scans and with the full report.
